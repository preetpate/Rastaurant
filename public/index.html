Our story

A neighbourhood kitchen with a coastal soul

Prime Restaurant began as a six-seat supper club in a converted garage. Twelve years on, the idea hasn’t changed: cook the food we grew up on, with the best of what the season gives us, and serve it like we’re feeding family.

Everything is made in house — the paneer, the pickles, the breads pulled from a clay tandoor at the pass. Our wine list leans low-intervention, and our bar leads with tamarind, curry leaf and smoke.

  • House-milled spices, ground every morning
  • Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free menus
  • Private dining for parties up to 40
